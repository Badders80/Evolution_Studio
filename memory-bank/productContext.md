# Evolution Stables - Product Context

## Syndication Business Model
Evolution Stables operates a digital syndication model for horse racing ownership. Key features:
- **Markets**: New Zealand, Dubai, Hong Kong
- **Fractional Ownership**: Users can buy shares of racehorses
- **Racehorse Selection**: Focus on proven bloodlines and trainers
- **Revenue Share**: Owners receive a share of prize money and breeding rights

## Content Strategy
The content strategy follows a "Fight Club" funnel approach:
1. **Build Audience First**: Create engaging horse racing content without mentioning ownership
2. **Reveal Ownership Model**: Once audience is established, introduce syndication opportunity
3. **Nurture Community**: Provide ongoing updates to keep owners engaged

## Content Types
### 1. Trainer Update
- Provides insights from the trainer about a horse's preparation
- Includes training footage and performance analysis
- Focus: Building connection with the horse and trainer

### 2. Race Preview
- Pre-race analysis including form guide, track conditions, and jockey stats
- Highlights key competitors and potential outcomes
- Hook: "The numbers don't lie"

### 3. Race Result
- Post-race coverage including highlights and analysis
- Celebrates wins and analyzes performance
- Includes owner updates and prize money information
